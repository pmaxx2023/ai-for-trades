1. Togal.AI

Best for: Commercial estimators. AI takeoff that auto-detects, measures and labels areas on plan sets, with change detection between revisions.

Pricing: Growth plan $299/mo per user, billed yearly. source

2. STACK

Best for: Subs & GCs wanting cloud takeoff. Cloud takeoff and estimating with AI-assisted measurement and collaboration.

Pricing: Takeoff/estimating plans 'as low as' $299 per user/mo; field plans from $49 per user/mo. source

3. Kreo

Best for: PDF/CAD/BIM takeoff. Cloud takeoff and estimating with AI-automated takeoff on PDF, CAD and image files. 7-day free trial.

Pricing: $100/user/mo (no AI), $175/user/mo (AI takeoff), $250/user/mo top tier, all billed annually. source

4. PlanSwift

Best for: Desktop/Excel-heavy estimators. Takeoff and estimating with Takeoff BOOST AI tools. 14-day free trial.

Pricing: Essential $2,000/yr per seat; Core $3,000/yr per seat. source

5. Beam AI

Best for: High-volume trade subs & suppliers. Automated AI takeoff with human QA; delivered takeoffs rather than DIY software.

Pricing: Not published on fetched page (unverified). source

6. Quotr.ai

Best for: Contractors and developers. AI takeoff from uploaded plans, with optional done-for-you pro formas.

Pricing: Not published on fetched page (unverified). source

7. Bluebeam

Best for: PDF markup & measurement. The industry-standard PDF markup and measurement tool; higher tiers add workflow automation.

Pricing: Per user, billed annually: $260, $330, $440, $590 tiers. source
