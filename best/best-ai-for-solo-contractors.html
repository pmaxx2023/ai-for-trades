1. Joist

Best for: Solo contractors: estimates & invoices. Mobile estimates, invoices and payments for contractors.

Pricing: Tiers from $12/mo up to $100/mo billed monthly (lower with annual billing). source

2. ServiceM8

Best for: Small trade businesses on iPhone. Job management for trades with AI-assisted features; priced by jobs per month.

Pricing: Free (1 user, 30 jobs/mo); Starter $29/mo; Growing $79/mo; Premium $149/mo; Premium Plus $349/mo. source

3. Jobber

Best for: Home service businesses, solo to 15+. Scheduling, quoting, invoicing and payments, with AI features for home service pros.

Pricing: Plans from $29 to $529/mo billed annually (per Jobber's pricing page); extra users $29/mo. source

4. Rosie

Best for: Solo owners & small businesses. 24/7 AI call answering with booking. Free trial.

Pricing: Professional $49/mo; Scale $149/mo; Growth $299/mo. source

5. Claude

Best for: Reading specs, scope checks, RFIs, emails. General AI assistant estimators use to read plans/specs text, compare vendor quotes and draft RFIs.

Pricing: Free; Pro $20/mo ($17/mo annual); Max from $100/mo. source

6. ChatGPT

Best for: Writing estimates, emails, marketing. General AI assistant for drafting proposals, customer emails, and job descriptions.

Pricing: Free tier available; paid plan prices render via JavaScript and were not read (unverified). source
